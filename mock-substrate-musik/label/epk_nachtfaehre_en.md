# Nachtfähre — Electronic Press Kit

Genre: Indie / Dream-Pop
Line-up: Lasse Detert (vocals, guitar), Momo Kwaśniewski (synths, production), Ilva Brandt (bass), Kim Sundermann (drums)
Based: Hamburg, Germany
Label: Fleetlicht Tonträger GmbH

## Bio

Nachtfähre formed in Hamburg and released their debut EP "Kai" in 2017. The band writes around Lasse Detert's guitar lines and vocals, with Momo Kwaśniewski's synth work pulling the songs toward something closer to dream-pop than straight indie rock. Ilva Brandt and Kim Sundermann round out a rhythm section that stays mostly out of the way until a track needs it to push.

Two EPs and one album in, the band still records most of its material together in the same room rather than passing files back and forth. Lyrics are in German. The sound sits somewhere between the guitar-driven end of German indie and the more electronic acts on the label's roster.

## Catalogue

- "Kai" (EP, 2017) — 5 tracks, debut release
- "Überfahrt" (EP, 2019) — 5 tracks
- "Landgang" (album, expected Q4 2026) — the band's first full-length, first new release since "Überfahrt"
